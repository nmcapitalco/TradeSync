What To Do Right Now
Three Steps While You Wait
1

Create a free Collective2 account

Collective2 is the platform that connects TradeSync's trades to your brokerage account. Creating an account is completely free — no credit card required. Go to collective2.com, click Sign Up, and use your email. Takes about 2 minutes. Once you have an account, find TradeSync in the strategy search and click Subscribe to activate your $49/month access.

2

Open your brokerage account — pick the right one for you

Your money stays in your own brokerage account at all times — TradeSync never touches it. You just need a compatible account connected to Collective2. If you're in the US: open Tradier — it's free, takes 10 minutes, and has zero commissions. If you're in Mexico: open an Interactive Brokers Pro account — they accept Mexican clients and have no monthly account fee, just small per-trade costs. Not sure which? We'll tell you exactly which one fits your situation when we reply.

Open Tradier (US) → Open IBKR Pro (Mexico / International) →
3

Fund your brokerage account

Once your brokerage account is approved — usually the same day for Tradier, 1–2 days for IBKR — transfer funds via bank ACH. We recommend a minimum of $10,000 so your account can mirror TradeSync's position sizes proportionally. You can start with less, but larger accounts execute more cleanly. The transfer is free and takes 1–3 business days to settle.

⏱ Takes 1–3 business days to fund — start now
4

Connect and activate AutoTrade — then you're done forever

Once your brokerage is funded, go back to your Collective2 account, open the TradeSync strategy page, and click "AutoTrade this strategy." Select your broker, authorize the connection, set your scaling to 1.0, and click Activate. From that moment forward, every TradeSync trade executes automatically in your account. You never have to do anything again. Our full step-by-step guide walks you through every single click.

What To Expect
Here's What Copy Trading
Actually Feels Like
📱

You Get an Email When a Trade Fires

Every time TradeSync places a trade, Collective2 sends you an email notification. You'll see exactly what was bought or sold, at what price, and in what size — after it's already executed in your account automatically. No action needed from you.

🔁

Trades Are Proportional To Your Balance

If TradeSync puts 8% of its model portfolio into a trade, Collective2 puts 8% of your account into the same trade. Your account size determines your position size — not ours. You stay in full control of how much of your account is used via the scaling factor.

🏦

Your Money Never Leaves Your Account

TradeSync and Collective2 cannot withdraw funds, move money, or access your cash. They only send trade signals. Your brokerage holds everything. You can log in, check your balance, and withdraw anytime — completely independent of TradeSync.

📅

Trades Are Infrequent — That's By Design

TradeSync is a swing trading strategy, not a day trading strategy. Trades happen when the right setup appears — not on a fixed schedule. Some weeks may have multiple trades. Some may have none. This is normal, expected, and intentional. Quality over quantity.

🛑

Cancel Anytime — No Commitment

If you ever want to stop, log into Collective2 and cancel your subscription. AutoTrade stops immediately. Any open positions in your brokerage stay open — you can close them manually or hold them. No penalty. No lock-in. No questions asked.

✉️

We're Here If You Have Questions

We'll respond to your message within 24 hours in English or Spanish. If you get stuck anywhere in setup, email nmcapitalco@gmail.com and we'll walk you through it personally. No bots — real replies from the people behind TradeSync.
